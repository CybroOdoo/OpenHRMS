Configure User Access Rights - Service Executor Role

Under Settings > Users & Companies > Users, assign the "Service Executor" security privilege to technicians or administrators. This role grants specialized access to the Service Execution workflow and task verification.

Centralized Services Navigation in Open HRMS

Inside the Employees module, the dedicated "Services" top-bar menu provides direct navigation to "Request Service" for creating and managing requests, and "Service Execution" for technicians to monitor their active assignments.

Service Request Creation & Instant Submission

Employees fill in the service subject, service category (e.g. Repair, Replace, Updating), product/asset item, service date, deadline, and priority. Clicking the "Submit" button submits the ticket for manager assignment.

Executor Assignment & Automatic Execution Record Generation

In the "Requested" stage, an authorized HR or service manager designates the technician under "Assigned To" and clicks "Assign". This transitions the ticket to "Assigned" and automatically generates a synchronized Service Execution record.

Service Execution & Technical Verification (Checked)

The assigned executor opens the task under Services > Service Execution, documents diagnostic observations in internal notes, finishes the maintenance work, and clicks "Checked" to complete execution and alert the manager.

Manager Final Review: Approval or Rejection

Once verified as "Checked", the manager reviews execution details, accesses execution records via the "Executions" smart button, reviews chatter logs, and clicks "Approve" to close the ticket or "Reject" if further action is required.

Open HRMS Service Request is an internal ticketing and maintenance management module for Odoo 20. It empowers employees to submit IT support, equipment repairs, software updates, or administrative service requests. Managers can review requests, assign dedicated technicians, track completion through linked execution records, and grant final approval.

Once a service request is submitted, it moves to the Requested stage. An authorized manager designates a technician in the Assigned To field and clicks Assign. This moves the ticket to Assigned and automatically creates a corresponding Service Execution record for that technician. The technician inspects the asset, completes the required work, and clicks Checked. Finally, the manager reviews the execution and clicks Approve or Reject.

Under Settings → Users & Companies → Users, users granted the Service Executor role receive access to the Service Execution menu item under Employees → Services. They can view their assigned service tasks, record diagnostic notes, and click the Checked button without requiring full HR manager permissions.

Yes. If an administrator or manager has been assigned the Service Executor privilege, they can be selected as the executor for any service request. They will have full ability to open the execution record, mark it as Checked upon completing the task, and subsequently approve the final request.

Managers can navigate to Employees → Configuration → Services → Service Categories. The system comes pre-loaded with standard categories (Repair, Replace, Updating, Checking, Adjustment, and Other). You can create custom categories, archive unused categories, and provide descriptive guidelines for your team.

When creating a request, users define the Service Date and Deadline Date with automatic validation preventing the deadline from preceding the service date. In both Service Request and Service Execution list views, any open ticket whose deadline has passed is dynamically highlighted with a red alert decoration.
