Open HRMS Employee History automatically records and visualizes key milestones throughout an employee's tenure at your organization. It tracks job position changes, department transfers, wage adjustments, contract modifications, and hourly cost evolution in a chronological timeline view.

Open any employee record in the Employees app and click on the dedicated Employee History tab. The visual timeline widget displays all career changes in reverse-chronological order, with clear indicators of what was changed, the previous value, and the new value.

The module automatically listens for modifications to:
  • Job Position & Department: Promotions, lateral shifts, and department transfers.
  • Salary: Wage increments, appraisals, and compensation revisions.
  • Contract: Renewals, contract type adjustments, and status changes.
  • Hourly Cost: Labor rate modifications affecting timesheet billing.

Yes! The timeline includes interactive category filter pills directly above the feed. With a single click, you can isolate events by All, Salary, Contract, Job & Department, or Hourly Cost without reloading the page.

When an employee's hourly cost changes over time, historical records preserve what their rate was during past project milestones. This ensures historical project profitability and timesheet valuation remain accurate and auditable.
