Open HRMS Employee Appraisal provides a structured multi-tier performance evaluation framework. HR managers or employees initiate an appraisal, select appropriate survey questionnaires for different participant groups (Managers, Employee Self-Review, Collaborators, Colleagues), set a deadline, and dispatch automated invitations. Once answers are submitted, managers review aggregated responses, schedule a final interview, and record conclusive evaluation notes.

The appraisal plan supports four independent evaluation tiers:
  • Managers: Direct supervisors or department heads with a dedicated Manager Opinion Form.
  • Employee: The employee being appraised submits their own Self-Appraisal Form.
  • Collaborators: Cross-functional team members or project partners with a Collaborator Opinion Form.
  • Colleagues: Peer coworkers who provide peer feedback via a Colleague Opinion Form.

Under Settings → Users, HR personnel should be assigned:
  1. Appraisal: Manager (under Human Resources) to create, manage, and close appraisals for all employees.
  2. Surveys: Administrator (under Marketing) to create, customize, and edit the survey questionnaire forms.
Standard employees with the "Appraisal: Employee" role can view and initiate appraisals only for their own profile.

When the appraisal manager clicks Start Appraisal And Send Forms, the module generates a unique, tokenized response URL for each selected reviewer. It automatically creates and dispatches personalized emails to each participant's work email address containing the survey link and the response deadline.

If a reviewer clicks their survey link after the appraisal has been marked as Done, Cancelled, or if it is still in To Start (Draft), the system renders a dedicated informative page informing them that the appraisal is closed or not currently accepting responses.

Managers can click the Answers smart button directly on the appraisal form (or navigate to Appraisal → Answers) to review all completed survey submissions. After reviewing ratings and comments, clicking Done unlocks the Final Evaluation tab to record summary notes, action items, and the scheduled Final Interview date.
