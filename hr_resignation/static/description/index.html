Open HRMS Resignation is a complete offboarding management suite for Odoo 20. It covers the entire employee separation journey from resignation request creation and multi-level approvals to departmental clearance, custody recovery, exit surveys, and automated Full & Final settlement.

When an employee or HR submits a resignation request, it enters the Draft state. Once confirmed, it escalates to the Department Manager who can approve, withdraw, or reject it. Following manager approval, the request advances to the HR Department for final validation, clearance kickoff, and settlement processing.

Administrators can define clearance templates by department (e.g. IT, Finance, Operations, HR). Each item is assigned a responsible officer. Clearance progress is tracked with an active progress bar, ensuring all equipment, badges, and dues are verified prior to final sign-off.

The module integrates with hr_custody to automatically list all company assets assigned to the employee. If an asset is unreturned, the clearance line is flagged as Blocked, and any agreed compensation due can be passed directly to the settlement computation as an asset recovery deduction.

With a single click on "Compute Settlement", the module calculates pending wages, loan deductions, salary advance recoveries, and asset recovery charges. Once approved, it generates a linked final settlement payslip in hr_payroll_community containing specific deduction salary rules.

Clicking "Relieve Employee" updates the employee record by setting the Departure Date, Resignation Date, and marking the departure reason as Resigned. The employee contract is properly ended and recorded in the audit history.
