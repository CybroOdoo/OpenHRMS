Key highlights

Comprehensive vacation management designed to ensure seamless business continuity while managing employee annual leaves.

Pending Task Handover & Delegation

Empower employees to record pending works directly on their leave request and allow managers to reassign them to available team members before approving the vacation.

Department Overlapping Leave Detection

Instantly identify other approved leaves within the same department during the requested period to prevent staffing shortages and maintain productivity.

Integrated Flight Ticket Booking

Book employee travel tickets directly from approved leaves with class, departure, and return flight details tracked in dedicated records.

Automated Accounting Vendor Bill

Confirming a flight ticket automatically generates a draft vendor bill for the airline partner linked with the configured travel expense account.

Leave Salary Calculation in Payslip

Dedicated salary rule dynamically computes and credits vacation allowance (Basic or Gross) when the Leave Salary checkbox is enabled on employee payslips.

Automated Pre-Vacation Email Reminders

Scheduled cron notifications automatically alert employees and managers prior to scheduled vacation start dates to prepare pending workflows.

Available Leave Balance & Pending Works Management

Track remaining legal leaves directly on the leave request form and log active project tasks that require reassignment during the vacation period.

Department Overlapping Leaves Analysis

Inspect department colleagues who have already booked approved leaves across overlapping dates to ensure adequate team coverage.

Task Re-Assignment During Leave Approval

When approving the leave, a dedicated modal prompts managers to delegate all pending tasks to available department team members.

Integrated Flight Ticket Booking Wizard

Book round-trip or one-way travel tickets from the approved leave with departure, destination, travel dates, and cabin class.

Confirmed Flight Ticket & Accounting Vendor Bill

Flight ticket sequence is tracked through its lifecycle, automatically generating a draft accounting vendor bill accessible via the Invoice smart button.
