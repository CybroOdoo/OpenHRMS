Open HRMS Core is the flagship master suite that integrates all fundamental Open HRMS components into one seamless human capital management system for Odoo 20. It brings executive dashboards, payroll, accounting vouchers, employee loans, salary advances, document expiry tracking, resignation offboarding, reminders, and corporate announcements together into a unified experience.

Open HRMS Core automatically loads and coordinates key modules including HRMS Dashboard, HR Payroll Community, Employee Loan Management & Accounting, Salary Advance, Employee Document Expiry, HR Reminders Todo, HR Resignation, HR Reward & Warning Announcements, HR Leave Request Aliasing, and HR Custody.

The suite connects payroll calculations with Odoo's financial double-entry bookkeeping. Monthly salary rules, employee payslip batches, loan installment deductions, and advance salary settlements automatically create and post balanced journal entries to designated expense and payable accounts.

A dedicated headphone support icon is integrated directly into the top navigation bar. Administrators and managers can click this button at any time to open a modal ticket submission dialog, enter category and priority details, attach screenshots, and transmit inquiries directly to Cybrosys technical support.
