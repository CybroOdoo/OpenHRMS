The Wages Protection System (WPS) is an electronic salary transfer system initiated by the UAE Central Bank and Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MOHRE). It ensures employees receive agreed salaries on time through approved banks and financial institutions.

A SIF file is the standardized data file submitted monthly by employers to their corporate bank. It contains Employee Detail Records (EDR) specifying each worker's wage and account info, followed by a Salary Control Record (SCR) with employer totals.

Every participating commercial bank and WPS exchange agent in the UAE has a unique 9-digit code assigned by the UAE Central Bank. Our module automatically pads shorter inputs to 9 digits to prevent portal validation errors.
