Open HRMS Official Announcements is an all-in-one HR communication suite for Odoo 20 that enables organizations to manage company broadcasts, employee reward letters, policy notices, and disciplinary warnings in one centralized place.

You can create General Announcements (visible to all staff) or targeted notices directed to specific Employees, Departments, or Job Positions. Each announcement can also be assigned a category such as Reward, Warning, or custom categories.

When an announcement is drafted, clicking "Send For Approval" moves it to the "Waiting For Approval" state. Authorized HR managers can review the content, target audience, and attachments before choosing to either Approve or Refuse the announcement.

Once an announcement is approved, employees viewing it can click the "Acknowledge" button. The system registers their name and timestamp under the Acknowledgments tab, giving HR administrators transparent tracking of employee engagement.

Targeted employees receive an automatic email notification upon announcement approval. Additionally, active announcements are highlighted in the Odoo Systray menu, allowing staff to view notices immediately from any screen.
