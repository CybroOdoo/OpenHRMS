Open HRMS Saudi GOSI is a specialized payroll localization module for Odoo 20 Community Edition. It automates General Organization for Social Insurance (GOSI) compliance by calculating employee deductions, employer contributions, managing eligibility criteria, and integrating directly with employee payslips.

The module computes employee GOSI contribution as a 9% deduction based on the employee's basic wage and housing allowance: result = -((contract.wage + (contract.wage * 3 / 12)) * 9 / 100).

The company contribution is calculated at 11%, combining 9% for annuities and 2% for occupational hazards insurance: result = (((contract.wage + (contract.wage * 3 / 12)) * 9 / 100) + (contract.wage * 2 / 100)).

The employee's age is calculated dynamically from their date of birth. When an employee is between 18 and 60 years old and marked as Saudi, the 'Eligible For GOSI' flag is automatically enabled and the GOSI Information tab becomes visible.

The module provides two structures: the Base Salary Structure For Saudi Workers (which includes the employee and company GOSI contribution rules) and the Base Salary For Overseas Workers (which excludes GOSI rules).
