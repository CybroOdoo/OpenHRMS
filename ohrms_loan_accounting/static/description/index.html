Open HRMS Loan Accounting extends the Open HRMS Loan module by automatically synchronizing employee loans with Odoo 20 Accounting. It generates double-entry journal entries upon loan disbursement, allows Account Manager approvals with configurable threshold limits, and accurately books principal recovery and interest income during monthly payroll runs.

When the Approval from Accounting Department setting is enabled in Employees Settings, loan requests meeting or exceeding the Approval Threshold transition into the Waiting Approval stage after HR approval. An Account Manager then reviews the financial details and clicks the Finance Approve button to release funds and generate the disbursement journal entry.

Upon final approval (or Finance approval), the system automatically creates and posts an accounting move:
  • Debit: Employee Loan Receivable Account (Current Asset) for the total loan amount.
  • Credit: Selected Loan Journal's Default Account (Bank / Cash) for the disbursed amount.

During payslip confirmation, the module intercepts the loan deduction rule (Rule Code: LO). It performs an automatic split:
  • Principal Portion: Credited to the Loan Receivable Account (reducing outstanding employee debt).
  • Interest Portion: Credited to the Interest Income Account (recognizing financial revenue).

Yes. Under Employees Settings → HRMS Loan, you can configure an Approval Threshold (e.g., $3,000.00). Any loan strictly below this amount is approved immediately upon HR sign-off, bypassing the Account Manager approval queue while still posting the proper disbursement journal entries.

When executing a Top-Up Loan action, the system generates an additional accounting journal move for the top-up disbursement amount (Debiting Loan Receivable and Crediting the Bank account) before recalculating the combined amortization schedule.

The module includes a built-in validation safeguard: if an employee's loan installment exceeds their net wage resulting in negative pay, Odoo raises a clear UserError alerting the payroll manager to adjust the deduction or defer the installment, safeguarding payroll integrity.
