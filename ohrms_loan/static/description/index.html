Key highlights

A robust, automated employee loan management system designed to handle loan requests, multi-tier approvals, interest computations, and payroll deductions.

Loan Request Management

Employees can easily create and track loan applications with requested amounts, installment counts, and repayment start dates.

Multi-Level Approvals

Streamlined approval hierarchy allowing managers and HR officers to review terms, verify schedules, and approve or refuse requests.

Flexible Interest Calculations

Supports both Flat Rate and Reducing Balance interest models with automated computation of interest amounts per installment.

Automated Amortization Schedule

Generates full payment schedules in a single click, detailing payment dates, principal amounts, interest breakdown, and line totals.

Early Settlements, Deferments & Top-Ups

Empower HR and employees with built-in wizards to settle loans early, defer installments with valid reasons, or top up existing balances.

Automated Payroll Deductions

Directly connects with Odoo 20 Payroll to pull due installments into employee payslips and automatically deduct them via salary rules.

Salary Deduction Rule Configuration

Configure a loan deduction rule (Rule Code: LO, Category: Deduction) under Salary Rules and assign it to the employee Salary Structure to ensure automatic payslip deductions.

Loan Request Navigation

Navigate to Loans & Advances → Request for Loan under the Employees module to view all loan applications or initiate a new loan request.

Create Loan Request

Submit a new loan request with employee details, loan amount, number of installments, payment start date, and interest configuration.

Flexible Interest Configurations

Configure the interest rate and choose between Flat Rate or Reducing Balance to automatically calculate the interest amount for each installment period.

Automated Installment Computation

Clicking 'Compute Installment' generates the complete amortization schedule, detailing the payment date, principal amount, interest amount, and line total for every cycle.

Loan Request Submission & Notifications

Upon submitting the loan request, automated email notifications instantly alert managers and HR personnel that a new request is awaiting review.

Manager Approval Workflow

HR Managers can review the requested amount, installment plan, and employee credentials, with easy one-click options to Approve or Refuse the request.

Advanced Loan Management Actions

Following approval, managers gain direct access to advanced actions: Early Settlement, Defer Installment, and Top-Up Loan directly from the active loan form.

Early Settlement Processing

Process early settlements seamlessly by choosing either to deduct the lump sum from next installment(s) or distribute the remaining balance equally across future periods.

Defer Installments

Support employee flexibility by postponing a specific monthly installment to a later date with a documented justification (e.g., medical emergency).

Loan Top-Ups

Easily top up an existing loan with new funds and additional installments, automatically recalculating and generating an updated unified payment schedule.

Employee Profile Integration

Directly access and audit all active, pending, and completed loans of any staff member using the Loans smart button on their Employee profile.

Automated Payslip Deduction

During payroll computation, due loan installments are automatically fetched and deducted from the employee's wage using the LO deduction salary rule.

Real-Time Loan Balance Tracking

Transparent, real-time totals on the loan record: Total Interest Amount, Total Amount, Total Paid Amount, Remaining Principal, and Outstanding Balance.

Allow Multiple Active Loans

Configure company-wide policies in Employees Settings, such as allowing multiple active loans simultaneously per employee.

Open HRMS Loan Management is a comprehensive module for Odoo 20 Community that automates employee loan requests, multi-level approvals, interest computations, payment deferments, top-ups, and salary deductions during monthly payroll processing.

The module automatically links with Odoo 20 HR Payroll via the LO (Loan Deduction) salary rule. When generating monthly payslips, pending installments scheduled for that month are automatically fetched and deducted from the employee's net wage.

The module supports two primary interest calculation methods: Flat Rate (interest computed evenly across the loan term) and Reducing Balance (interest calculated on the outstanding balance for each cycle).
