Open HRMS Advance Salary is a dedicated Odoo 20 Community module enabling employees to request advances against their upcoming salary. It automates eligibility calculations against active contracts, enforces managerial approvals, registers accounting disbursements, and recovers the amount via automated payslip deductions.

When an employee or HR officer initiates a request, the module retrieves the employee's monthly wage from their running contract. It then calculates the Maximum Allowed amount based on the company's percentage limit (e.g. 50%) and any fixed maximum limit. It subtracts any Already Outstanding unrecovered advances to determine the Remaining Eligible amount.

Once a manager approves the request, a Register Payment button appears. Clicking it opens a payment wizard that generates and posts an outbound accounting payment (`account.payment`), crediting the selected Bank/Cash Journal and debiting the Employee Advance Receivable Account, while marking the advance state as Paid.

During monthly payslip generation, the module automatically retrieves unpaid advances for that employee and adds an input line under Other Inputs (Code: SAR). The salary structure computes the deduction, reducing the net salary paid to the employee.

When the employee's payslip is validated and marked as done, the system updates the Deducted Amount on the advance. If the remaining Balance Amount reaches zero, the advance status automatically switches to Closed with a green ribbon badge.
