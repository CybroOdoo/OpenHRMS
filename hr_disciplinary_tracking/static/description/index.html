The Disciplinary Tracking module provides an organized, transparent framework for monitoring and addressing employee policy violations. It ensures that disciplinary procedures are conducted fairly by providing employees the opportunity to explain allegations before any corrective action is finalized.

The workflow proceeds in four structured steps:
  1. Draft: An incident is registered with the employee name, department, joined date, and disciplinary reason.
  2. Waiting Explanation: The HR Manager clicks "Proceed", prompting the employee to provide their statement.
  3. Waiting Action: The employee submits their explanation and attachments for management review.
  4. Action Validated: The HR Manager selects an Action Category, specifies action details, and validates the case.

Under Employees → Disciplinary Actions → Discipline Category, HR Managers can define custom categories with a Code, Name, Details, and Category Type:
  • Disciplinary Category: Used as the initial violation reason (e.g. Absenteeism, Misconduct, Policy Breach).
  • Action Category: Used as the corrective action taken (e.g. Verbal Warning, Written Notice, Suspension).
