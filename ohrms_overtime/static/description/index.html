Configure Employee Overtime Wage Rates

In the employee contract settings under the Payroll tab, define the specific Day Wage and Hour Wage for the employee. These wage rates serve as the base for calculating cash overtime payments.

Configure Cash Overtime Rules

Navigate to Overtime Request > Overtime Types to create rules for Cash compensation with Hour or Day durations and define rate multiplier rules (e.g., 2.0× standard rate).

Configure Compensatory Leave Types

Configure Overtime Types for Leave compensation, linking the overtime request directly to specific time off types (such as Compensatory Days or Sick Leave).

Submit Overtime Request for Leave

Submit an overtime request by choosing Type as 'Leave' and Duration Type as 'Hour' or 'Days'. The total duration is calculated automatically based on the selected period.

Multi-Stage Approval Workflow

Move requests smoothly through Draft, Waiting, and Approved stages. Managers and HR officers have clear Approve and Refuse action controls with complete chatter audit tracking.

Instant Leave Allocation on Approval

Open HRMS Overtime is a specialized HR management module designed for Odoo 20 Community edition. It allows organizations to record, approve, calculate, and compensate employee overtime work through monetary payroll earnings or compensatory leave allocations.

When creating an overtime request, the user selects between Cash and Leave. If Cash is chosen, the calculated amount is automatically credited as an allowance on the employee's payslip. If Leave is chosen, approved overtime hours or days are automatically converted into credited time off balances in the Time Off app.

The calculation formula is: Wage Rate × Rate Multiplier × Duration. The Wage Rate (Hour Wage or Day Wage) is configured on the employee's contract. The Rate Multiplier is configured in the selected Overtime Type rules (for example, 1.5× or 2.0×).

Yes! The module includes built-in payroll integration with Odoo 20 HR Payroll (hr_payroll_community). When generating an employee payslip, all approved and unpaid cash overtime requests within the pay period are automatically aggregated under the OT100 salary rule.

Once a payslip containing overtime inputs is validated and posted, the corresponding overtime requests are flagged as is_payslip_paid = True and display a prominent green PAID IN PAYSLIP ribbon. This completely prevents subsequent payslips from pulling the same overtime records again.

Upon clicking Approve on a Leave-type overtime request, the system automatically creates a validated Time Off Allocation (hr.leave.allocation) in the Time Off app for the employee. The newly created allocation record is referenced directly on the overtime request form for complete traceability.

Yes. Employees can upload supporting files (timesheet proofs, client sign-offs, shift logs) via the "Attach A File" field or directly through the Odoo chatter panel attached to the overtime request.
